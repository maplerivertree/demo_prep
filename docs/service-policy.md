# Cascade Home Services — Service Policies

- Business hours: Monday–Saturday, 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
- After-hours emergencies: true emergencies only (active leaks, no heat
  in winter, gas smell). Gas smell: we advise the customer to leave the
  home and call the gas utility and 911 first, then us.
- Service area: greater Seattle area, up to 30 miles from downtown.
- Scheduling: appointments confirmed by a human coordinator. The agent
  never promises a specific time slot.
- Cancellation: free with 24 hours notice; $75 fee inside 24 hours.
- Complaints: acknowledged same business day, owner personally reviews
  within 48 hours. Escalate all complaints to the owner.
- Payment: due on completion; cards, checks, financing on jobs over $2,000.
- Warranty: 1 year on labor; manufacturer warranty on parts.
